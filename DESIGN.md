# Forklight design direction

Forklight is an editorial decision instrument, not a dashboard. Its visual language borrows from folded paper, print registration marks, and counterfactual branching diagrams. The page uses an ivory field, cobalt structure, vermilion calls to action, and deliberately oversized type.

The silhouette is a continuous vertical broadsheet: a typographic cover, an animated folding fan, a diagonal thesis, a circular decision compass, and a spatial decision canvas. The input surface is deliberately non-linear: the decision sits in a red central lens, baseline and intervention occupy asymmetric opposing fields, metric and horizon anchor the lower arc, and evidence forms a single dark provenance rail. It avoids numbered rows, tables, skeleton loaders, side-panel dashboards, and CausalLoom's linear composer grammar.
